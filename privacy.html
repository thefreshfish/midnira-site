Privacy

What this website collects about visitors, and what happens when you write to us. It is short because the site collects very little.

What the site collects

This site sets no cookies and has no forms or accounts.

It counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which records page views without cookies and does not follow you to other sites.

The pages are served by Cloudflare and the fonts load from Google Fonts. As with any web request, those two providers receive your IP address and browser details in order to deliver the files.

When you contact us

If you email info@midnira.ai, your message is kept in Midnira's business mailbox and used to reply to you.

If you book a call, the booking is handled by Calendly under its own privacy policy.

We do not sell contact details or share them for marketing.

Client data

The documents, systems and test results we handle for clients are covered by each client's written agreement, not by this page. The home page explains how your data is handled.

Questions

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, write to info@midnira.ai.
