One practice, pointed in two directions.

Running agents in production shows you how they fail. Breaking into systems shows you what failure costs. Midnira's operations work is built by a penetration tester, and its testing uses agents built by someone who runs them in production. The same three rules apply to both.

People approve what matters

Agents propose and people decide. The approval step is part of the design and cannot be switched off for convenience.

Try the morning queue
Code decides what must be right

Where there is one correct answer, such as a deadline, a filing rule or a scope boundary, it is enforced by code. Models are used where judgment helps.

Try the scope gate
Everything leaves a record

Each action is logged with its inputs and the person who approved it, so any result can be traced back to its source.
